01 — About Me

The Researcher
Behind the Exploits

Hi, I'm Srishti — a Web3 security researcher passionate about finding and documenting vulnerabilities in DeFi protocols before malicious actors do.

My journey started with Ethernaut, where I developed a deep understanding of EVM internals and common Solidity pitfalls. I then moved on to Damn Vulnerable DeFi, tackling complex multi-contract exploit scenarios involving flash loans, price manipulation, and governance attacks.

Today, I conduct in-depth protocol research — reading live codebases, mapping attack surfaces, and documenting potential vulnerabilities across DeFi, governance, NFT, and infrastructure contracts.
